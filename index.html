Privacy Policy
NASCENT CAUSE
Last updated: May 2026
What we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth — we never see your password in plain text).

When you use the platform we collect the information you enter — your organization's name, mission, board member information, state registration details, and bylaws questionnaire answers. We also store conversation transcripts from the founding conversation so you can return to them.

Why we collect it

Everything we collect is used to provide the service — to pre-populate your documents, generate your compliance checklist, and personalize the guidance Claude gives you. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it for any purpose other than operating Nascent Cause.

How we protect it

Your data is stored in Supabase with Row Level Security — meaning your records are only accessible to you. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use Netlify serverless functions to proxy all AI API calls so your data never goes directly to third-party servers from your browser.

Third parties

We use Supabase for database and authentication, Netlify for hosting, and Anthropic's Claude API to power the AI features. We use Stripe for payment processing. None of these providers receive your organization's content data — only the minimum necessary to operate their service (authentication tokens, payment information).

Your rights

You can request a copy of all data we hold about you, or request that your account and all associated data be permanently deleted. To make either request email us at info@nascentcause.com or use the deletion option below.

Cookies

We use only the cookies necessary for authentication — a session token that keeps you logged in. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@nascentcause.com

Delete my account

This will permanently delete your account and all associated data — your organization profile, board members, bylaws answers, conversation history, and generated documents. This cannot be undone.
